# Asset provenance and generation prompts

All five assets were created with the built-in image-generation tool for this
project. They are reference-inspired demo artwork, not authentic listing photos,
original reference exports, or screenshots of Flutter. No CLI/API fallback was used.
Original generated PNG outputs were preserved; delivery copies were re-encoded
and resized without compositional edits. The wordmark retains its alpha channel.

| Project path | Dimensions | Use |
|---|---|---|
| assets/images/night-coupe.webp | 960 × 1440 | Welcome, sample feed and BMW-style listing |
| assets/images/violet-coupe.webp | 1000 × 667 | Sample coupe sale listing and feed |
| assets/images/red-roadster.webp | 1000 × 667 | Sample roadster rental |
| assets/images/white-suv.webp | 1000 × 667 | Sample SUV rental |
| assets/brand/wordmark.png | 800 × 316 | Script brand header and welcome |

The synthetic cars may differ from exact real-world model specifications. Do not
use them to represent actual seller inventory. The logo is a proposed visual
interpretation, not an exact reconstruction of the reference's lettering.

## Welcome prompt

Create an original photorealistic promotional artwork asset for a Flutter mobile
app called Cars Night. Portrait 2:3 composition. A deep midnight-blue BMW-style
sport coupe viewed head on, low angle, luminous crisp blue-white headlights,
entire front visible, parked on rain-wet asphalt reflecting blue and magenta
lights. Futuristic modern city skyline behind with glowing blue and magenta
buildings, deep navy night sky. Center the car in the lower middle, skyline in
upper half. Premium automotive photography, sharp realistic vehicle geometry,
elegant rather than busy. No text, no wordmark, no interface, no phone frame,
no collage, no people, no watermark. This is a single welcome-screen background
inspired by the neon car marketplace reference.

## Violet coupe prompt

Single landscape 3:2 photorealistic automotive photograph for a sample Cars Night
app listing. A purple Lamborghini Huracan-style coupe, three-quarter front view,
entire car visible with space around it, parked on wet urban pavement at blue
hour, elegant city buildings and soft magenta/blue ambient accents. Sharp realistic
body, wheels and windshield. No text, no price, no UI, no collage, no people,
no watermark. This is illustrative demo imagery.

Single landscape 3:2 photorealistic automotive photograph for a sample Cars Night

whole vehicle visible with generous space around it, parked outside a refined
contemporary villa with greenery at dusk. Realistic geometry, black roof and

## Wordmark prompt

Create a clean typographic wordmark asset reading exactly "Cars Night" for a premium
neon automotive community app. Only these two words on a truly transparent
background. Wide horizontal composition, closely spaced words on one baseline.
Elegant energetic connected brush script with tall flowing capitals and slight
rightward lean, readable at small size. "Cars" is solid pure white; "Night" uses a
smooth left-to-right electric cyan through violet to bright magenta gradient.
Crisp smooth letter edges. No glow, no shadow, no car, no icons, no border,
no tagline, no decorative lines. Trim composition closely around the lettering
with small clean transparent padding.
